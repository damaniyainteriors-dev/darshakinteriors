# DARSHAK DAMANIYA INTERIOR — Civil, Fabrication & Luxury Interiors

Official website repository for **DARSHAK DAMANIYA INTERIOR**, a Mumbai-based firm specializing in **Luxury Interior Design & Fitouts, Structural Civil Construction, MS/SS Metal Fabrication, Custom Furniture, and Turnkey Property Renovations**.

---

## 🌟 Features & Highlights

- **Pure-Image Full-Width Hero Slider**: 5 high-resolution slides with auto-rotation, smooth progress indicator bar, manual arrows, and pagination controls.
- **Luxury White & Gold Theme**: Clean aesthetic matching the official branding identity (`#ffffff`, `#b8860b`, `#d4af37`, `#0f172a`).
- **Specialized Services**:
  - Luxury Interior Design & Fitouts
  - Heavy MS & SS 304/316 Metal Fabrication
  - Structural Civil Construction & RCC Works
  - Bespoke Handcrafted Wood & Metal Furniture
  - Turnkey Residential & Commercial Renovation
  - Heavy Industrial Warehouse Sheds & Mezzanines
- **Filterable Portfolio Gallery**: Interactive category filtering with fullscreen Lightbox zoom and instant WhatsApp inquiry triggers.
- **Interactive Project Cost Estimator**: Ballpark budgeting tool with square footage slider and quality tier selection (*Standard, Premium, Luxury*).
- **Direct WhatsApp & Phone Integration**: Instant quotation form generating formatted messages for `+91 98678 19387`.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Design**: Sticky mobile bottom quick-action bar, responsive drawer menu, and floating WhatsApp contact button.
