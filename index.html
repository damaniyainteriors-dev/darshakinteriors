MUMBAI'S TRUSTED EXPERTS IN INTERIOR, CIVIL & FABRICATION

Established in 2003 under the leadership of Darshak Damaniya, Darshak Damaniya Interior has delivered over two decades of premier interior design, civil construction, heavy structural metal fabrication, custom modular furniture, and turnkey renovation excellence across Mumbai and Maharashtra.

With over 20+ years of on-site experience and our central workshop located in Dahisar (East), Mumbai, we merge interior design aesthetics with strong civil and metal craftsmanship. Whether designing luxury residential interiors, executing commercial office spaces, casting RCC structures, or fabricating designer MS & SS gates and sheds, we stand firmly on our core promise: "Built on Trust. Delivered with Quality."
